# FFX Monster Arena — capture data

Data layer for the Monster Arena guide. Protocol and the meaning of each status:
[`README.md`](README.md). General arena facts live in [`ffx.md`](ffx.md) — the menu order,
the one-time ×99 reward lots, the `Unknown` menu entries, and the Species Conquest quota
rule are all recorded there and are **not** repeated here.

**Primary version:** HD Remaster / International.

**Legend used in every table below.** *"region-wide"* means the source only places the
fiend somewhere in the named area and gives no zone inside it — that is an honest gap, not
a shorthand. *"arena-filed under X"* means the arena counts that fiend toward area X even
though it also spawns elsewhere in the world; this cross-filing is a real trap and is
flagged wherever it occurs.

**Independent count baseline**, obtained before any area table was accepted and used to
check each one: Besaid 3 – Kilika 4 – Mi'ihen 8 – Mushroom Rock 7 – Djose 7 – Thunder
Plains 8 – Macalania 10 – Bikanel 6 – Calm Lands 9 – Stolen Fayth 9 – Mt. Gagazet 12 –
Inside Sin 9 – Omega Ruins 10. **Total 102.**

## Travel — which areas are NavMap destinations

**Nine land you directly:** Besaid – Kilika – Mi'ihen Highroad – Djose Road (via "Djose
Highroad and Temple") – Thunder Plains – Macalania – Bikanel – Calm Lands – Mt. Gagazet.

**Four need special handling:**

| Area | How to reach it |
|---|---|
| **Mushroom Rock Road** | Not a normal destination. Land at **Mi'ihen Highroad** or **Djose** and walk in. *(A "Mushroom Rock Road" entry exists behind the **GODHAND** password but it drops you in a hidden side-cavern, not the road — useless for capturing.)* |
| **Stolen Fayth Cavern** | Not a destination. Land at **Mt. Gagazet** and walk south, or take the Calm Lands' northeastern exit down into Gorge Bottom. |
| **Inside Sin** | The entry is called **Sin**, not "Inside Sin". Selecting it triggers the mandatory five-fight airship gauntlet first; only afterwards are you standing where the 9 fiends live. |
| **Omega Ruins** | Absent from the NavMap until you find it by coordinate search (see area 13). Afterwards it is a normal entry. |

- **Status:** confirmed for the routing of every area; a few exact on-screen NavMap
  spellings are single-source
- **Verified:** 2026-08-16
- **Sources:** https://jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X/Monster-Arena/ · https://jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X/Side-Quests/Airship-Passwords-and-Hidden-Coordinates.html · https://jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X/Walkthrough/31-Sin.html
- **Bikanel note:** **Home** cannot be re-entered after the story. Only the surrounding
  desert remains — which is exactly where the captures are.

## Baaj Temple is NOT an arena capture area

- **Answer:** **No.** It appears nowhere in the arena's 13 areas, and there is nothing
  capturable there — its only fightable creature is **Geosgaeno**, a boss, and bosses
  cannot be caught.
- **Status:** confirmed (two direct fetches of jegged's own arena index and Area Conquest
  pages, both of which omit it)
- **Verified:** 2026-08-16
- **Sources:** https://jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X/Monster-Arena/ · https://jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X/Monster-Arena/Area-Conquest/
- **Notes:** One search summary listed Baaj alongside the real areas; it contradicts two
  direct fetches and is treated as a misread, not a second position. Baaj's actual role is
  the **Anima** side quest and the optional Geosgaeno rematch — see `ffx.md`.
